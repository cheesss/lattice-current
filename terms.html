Terms of use

Not investment advice. Lattice Current produces statistical signals for research and decision support. It is NOT investment, legal, tax, or accounting advice. Nothing it produces is an offer or solicitation to buy or sell any security, instrument, or fund.

Use cases this is appropriate for

  • Surfacing event-driven coverage and statistical reaction patterns in public market data.
  • Comparing observed news intensity to baseline rates per theme.
  • Flagging mismatches between coverage volume and matched-control returns.
  • Internal research workflows that require an evidence-graded view of theme momentum.

Use cases this is NOT appropriate for

  • Automated trading without human review.
  • Investment recommendations to retail clients.
  • Compliance / regulatory filings (the underlying data is not warranted).
  • Real-time decisions where second-by-second latency matters (data refresh is delayed by source minimums and cache TTLs).

Data accuracy

The system aggregates third-party data. We do our best to flag staleness (modelTrust, freshness pillars, calibration warnings) but cannot guarantee end-to-end correctness. Specifically:

  • Article sources can publish corrections after we ingest.
  • Evidence grades depend on matched-control sufficiency that varies by theme.
  • Model predictions can become stale — see the modelTrust banner before acting.
